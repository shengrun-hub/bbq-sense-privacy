简体中文

BBQ-Sense 由 FUZHOU OEAST ELECTRONIC CO.,LTD.（以下简称“我们”）提供，用于连接兼容的蓝牙烧烤温度计、查看食物或环境温度、设置烹饪提醒和制作分享照片。本政策说明 Android 应用及本隐私政策网页如何处理信息。温度计读数用于烹饪，不是人体健康或医疗测量。

1. 我们处理的信息及用途

  • 蓝牙设备与温度数据：为扫描、识别、连接和重新连接温度计，应用访问附近兼容设备的名称、蓝牙地址、信号强度及连接状态，并接收探针温度。烹饪记录、温度设置、食物预设及计时设置在手机本地处理和保存，用于显示曲线、温度监测和报警。应用没有用于上传这些记录的自建服务器，也不要求注册账户。
  • 相机与照片：仅在使用拍照功能时访问相机，用于拍摄照片及添加温度等水印。生成的照片会保存在应用缓存中，可能被后续拍照覆盖。应用不会自行把照片上传到我们的服务器。
  • 可选的照片定位：在您使用照片位置功能并同意相关说明、授予定位权限后，应用通过高德定位 SDK 获取当前位置及地址，用于照片位置水印。定位可能涉及精确或大致位置，以及辅助定位所需的网络和设备信息；详见第 3 节。拒绝定位不影响蓝牙测温，仍可使用不含位置的拍照功能。应用不申请后台定位权限。
  • 本地诊断：应用可在私有存储中记录崩溃堆栈等错误信息，用于排查故障；我们未设置自动上传这些本地崩溃日志的服务。
  • 联系信息：如果您主动发邮件联系我们，我们会处理您提供的邮箱、问题描述和附件，用于回复及处理请求。请勿发送与问题无关的敏感信息。

2. 设备权限及您的选择

  • 蓝牙／附近设备：用于扫描、连接温度计及接收温度；拒绝后无法使用对应连接功能。在较早的 Android 系统上，蓝牙扫描还可能需要定位权限和系统定位开关。
  • 位置：用于您主动选择的照片位置功能，以及旧版 Android 要求的蓝牙扫描授权。Android 12 及以上的蓝牙扫描声明为不用于推导物理位置。
  • 相机：用于您主动拍摄照片。旧版 Android（Android 9 及以下）还可能请求存储权限，以支持照片文件处理。
  • 通知、前台服务及保持唤醒：用于显示设备连接与监测状态、在应用切换至后台时继续连接温度计，以及发出温度或倒计时提醒。蓝牙监测前台服务用于已连接设备，不用于后台定位。
  • 网络及 Wi-Fi 状态：用于可选的高德网络定位及相关连接状态判断。

您可以在 Android“设置 → 应用 → BBQ-Sense → 权限”中调整授权，在应用中断开设备或结束监测。撤回权限可能使相应功能不可用，不影响撤回前已经进行的处理。关闭通知可能影响提醒的可见性。

3. 第三方服务与信息提供

高德定位 SDK：提供方为北京高德图强科技有限公司，用于可选的照片定位与地址解析。定位过程中，SDK 可将位置及辅助定位信息发送到高德的服务端。根据所用功能、设备、系统权限和 SDK 配置，涉及的信息包括经纬度、GNSS 信息、Wi-Fi 状态及热点信息（如 SSID、BSSID、信号强度）、基站信息、IP 地址、网络类型、运营商信息、设备品牌与型号、操作系统、屏幕分辨率、应用名称及版本，以及辅助定位传感器信息。SDK 对这些信息的处理、保存及权利行使方式详见高德地图开放平台隐私权政策。仅清除本应用数据不会自动删除第三方已依法处理的数据。

您主动分享照片：当您选择邮件、Facebook、Twitter 或其他可用分享应用时，系统会把您选定的照片及临时读取权限交给相应应用；照片可能包含温度和您选择添加的位置水印。分享前请检查内容。接收方随后如何处理信息，由其隐私政策及您的操作决定。

我们不出售您的个人信息，也不将蓝牙温度记录或照片用于广告画像。Android、设备厂商及您使用的第三方应用可能按照其各自政策处理系统诊断、备份或分享信息。

4. 保存、备份与删除

温度记录、设置和本地错误日志保存在应用的本地存储中；缓存照片可能被覆盖或由系统清理。您可以使用应用提供的删除功能，或在 Android 设置中清除应用存储、卸载应用，以删除本机应用私有数据。单独清除缓存不会删除所有设置或数据库记录。

本应用允许 Android 系统备份。根据您的系统、设备和备份设置，部分应用数据可能由您选择的系统备份服务保存，并在重新安装时恢复；请通过相应备份服务管理或删除备份。已经分享、导出或另行保存的照片，以及第三方收到的数据，不会随本应用卸载自动删除，请在相应应用或服务中处理。

对于您主动发送的联系邮件，我们仅在处理咨询、争议及履行适用义务所需期间保留。您可以通过下方邮箱提出查询、纠正或删除请求；我们会结合适用法律及必要的身份核验处理。

5. 安全措施

我们通过 Android 应用私有存储和权限机制限制数据访问；分享照片时通过系统分享机制提供必要的文件访问权限。请保护手机、系统账户及备份账户，并谨慎选择分享接收方。设备与网络服务均不能保证绝对安全。我们不会要求您通过联系邮件提供账户密码。

6. 儿童、政策更新与网页托管

本应用是一般用途的烹饪温度计工具，并非专门面向儿童。若您认为儿童通过联系渠道向我们提供了需要删除的个人信息，请联系我们。

当功能或信息处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页日期和内容，并在适用情况下提供额外说明或征求同意。

English

BBQ-Sense is provided by FUZHOU OEAST ELECTRONIC CO.,LTD. ("we", "us"). It connects to compatible Bluetooth cooking thermometers, displays food or ambient temperature, provides cooking alerts, and creates photos for sharing. This policy covers the Android app and this privacy policy website. Thermometer readings are for cooking, not human health or medical measurements.

1. Information we process and why

  • Bluetooth devices and temperature: To discover, identify, connect and reconnect to thermometers, the app accesses nearby compatible devices' names, Bluetooth addresses, signal strength and connection status, and receives probe readings. Cooking records, temperature settings, food presets and timer settings are processed and stored locally on your phone for charts, monitoring and alerts. We do not operate an app server for uploading these records, and no account registration is required.
  • Camera and photos: The app accesses the camera when you use the photo feature, to take pictures and add watermarks such as temperature. Generated photos are stored in the app cache and may be overwritten by later photos. The app does not automatically upload photos to our servers.
  • Optional photo location: When you use the photo location feature, accept its disclosure and grant location permission, the app uses the AMap Location SDK to obtain your current location and address for a photo watermark. This may involve precise or approximate location and supporting network and device information, as described in section 3. Declining location does not prevent Bluetooth temperature monitoring or taking photos without a location watermark. The app does not request background location permission.
  • Local diagnostics: The app may store error information such as crash stack traces in its private storage for troubleshooting. We have not configured a service to upload these local crash logs automatically.
  • Contact information: If you email us, we process your email address, message and attachments to respond and handle your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information unrelated to your enquiry.

2. Permissions and your choices

  • Bluetooth / Nearby devices: Used to discover and connect to thermometers and receive readings. Denying permission prevents the corresponding connection features. Earlier Android versions may also require location permission and the system location switch for Bluetooth scanning.
  • Location: Used for photo location that you choose to enable and for Bluetooth scanning permission required by older Android versions. On Android 12 and later, Bluetooth scanning is declared as not used to derive physical location.
  • Camera: Used when you choose to take a photo. Older Android versions (Android 9 and below) may also request storage permission for photo file handling.
  • Notifications, foreground service and wake lock: Used to show connection and monitoring status, maintain a thermometer connection while the app is in the background, and provide temperature or countdown alerts. The Bluetooth monitoring foreground service is for connected devices, not background location.
  • Network and Wi-Fi state: Used for optional AMap network location and related connection status checks.

You can change permissions in Android Settings → Apps → BBQ-Sense → Permissions, and disconnect devices or end monitoring in the app. Revoking a permission may disable its associated feature and does not affect processing already performed. Disabling notifications may make alerts less visible.

3. Third-party services and disclosures

AMap Location SDK: Provided by Beijing Gaode Tuqiang Technology Co., Ltd. (北京高德图强科技有限公司), for optional photo location and address lookup. During location requests, the SDK may transmit location and supporting information to AMap's servers. Depending on the feature, device, system permissions and SDK configuration, this includes coordinates, GNSS information, Wi-Fi status and access point information (such as SSID, BSSID and signal strength), cell tower information, IP address, network type, carrier information, device brand and model, operating system, screen resolution, app name and version, and sensor information supporting location. See the AMap Open Platform Privacy Policy for its processing, retention and privacy request procedures. Clearing this app's data does not automatically erase data already lawfully processed by a third party.

Photos you choose to share: When you select email, Facebook, Twitter or another available sharing app, Android provides the selected photo and temporary read access to that app. The photo may include temperature and any location watermark you chose to add. Check its contents before sharing. The recipient's subsequent processing is governed by its policy and your choices.

We do not sell your personal information or use Bluetooth temperature records or photos for advertising profiles. Android, device manufacturers and third-party apps you use may handle system diagnostics, backups or shared content under their own policies.

4. Retention, backup and deletion

Temperature records, settings and local error logs are kept in the app's local storage. Cached photos may be overwritten or cleared by the system. You can use available in-app deletion controls, clear app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its private data from your phone. Clearing the cache alone does not delete all settings or database records.

The app allows Android system backup. Depending on your system, device and backup settings, some app data may be stored by your selected system backup provider and restored after reinstallation. Manage or delete backups through that service. Photos you have shared, exported or saved separately, and data held by third parties, are not automatically deleted when you uninstall this app; manage them through the relevant app or service.

We retain emails you send us only as needed to handle enquiries, disputes and applicable obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion using the contact below. We handle requests subject to applicable law and necessary identity verification.

5. Security

We use Android's private app storage and permission controls to restrict access, and its sharing mechanism to provide necessary file access when you share photos. Protect your phone, system and backup accounts, and choose sharing recipients carefully. No device or network service can guarantee absolute security. We will not ask you to email account passwords.

6. Children, updates and website hosting

The app is a general-purpose cooking thermometer tool and is not specifically directed to children. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information to us through our contact channels that should be deleted.

We update this page and its date when features or data practices change, and provide additional notices or seek consent where appropriate.
